Methodology. Pairs at product-color grain (Norce variant parent), same-order co-purchase over the last 12 months, statistical floor 30 co-purchases. Attach = orders with both ÷ orders with the anchor (the higher-volume product). Lift = observed co-purchase ÷ expected if independent. Open value = gap orders × companion median unit price.
