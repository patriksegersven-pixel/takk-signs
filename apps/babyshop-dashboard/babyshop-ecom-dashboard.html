On-site search
What visitors type into the search box (view_search_results events). Rising terms are demand you can meet with a landing page, a synonym or stock.
On-site search is not tracked in GA4. The site fires almost no view_search_results events, so search terms, search usage and what people fail to find cannot be reported. See tracking gaps for the fix.

How to read this
Four different things are counted on this page — sessions, events, items and users. They do not divide into each other, and a rate is only comparable with another rate on the same basis.
