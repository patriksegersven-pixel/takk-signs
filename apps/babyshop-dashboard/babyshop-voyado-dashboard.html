Read these first. “Sent” is not “delivered” — the Voyado share carries no bounce or delivery-status data, so there is no deliverability rate here. Open rate is inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection pre-fetching images; lead on click rate. Revenue is last-click within 7 days, an upper bound on email's contribution — not incrementality, because only 25 of 737,751 sendouts run a control group. Transactional mail is excluded throughout.
