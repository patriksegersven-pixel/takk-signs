Share of Search
—
How to read this tab. The headline share is babyshop ÷ (babyshop + competitor retailer brand searches) — navigational terms only, tiered hyper (broad baby/kids specialists) + core (premium/functional kidswear); the toggle adds kid-scoped fast-fashion terms. Category demand is a separate curated basket and is never in the share denominator — adding generic keywords would move the "share" without anything real changing. Volumes are Google Keyword Planner monthly averages per market, refreshed monthly on the 15th.
Share of search by market
3-month rolling share of tracked retailer brand searches. Sweden's history is seeded from the old manual sheet (back to Sep 2020); other markets start with the API window (~4 years).
Retailer race
Each retailer's share of the tracked navigational searches, 3-month rolling.
Category demand vs own brands
Monthly search volume: the curated category basket (market potential + seasonality) and the private-label brands (Kuling, Stoy, Buddy & Hope, NG Baby, Carena, Little Jalo).
Category buckets
Each bucket aggregates its 2–3 canonical terms. Indexed mode rebases every bucket's 12-month rolling volume to 100 at its first complete year, so trajectories compare across buckets of very different size; volume mode shows raw monthly searches (3-month rolling). The chips above filter these panels, the category KPI and chart, and the keyword table.
