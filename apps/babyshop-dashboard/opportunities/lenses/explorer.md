# Lens: Explorer

Question: what is nobody looking for? Find opportunities that fall between
the fixed lenses. Run AFTER the other lenses and read the ledger extract in
your brief first — work only where nothing has been proposed.

You have the highest risk of false findings, because scanning many
dimensions always turns up patterns. The evidence bar in RULES.md is a hard
gate here: no replication, no candidate. Return at most three candidates.

## Methods

1. **Cross-domain joins** no single lens owns — e.g. stock-outs × ad spend,
   returns × brand × size, first-purchase product × later value, discount
   depth × repeat rate, delivery mode × repeat rate, channel of acquisition
   × 12-month value.
2. **Anomaly scan.** Segments (market × category × brand × channel ×
   customer type) performing far above or below what the rest of the
   business predicts. Then ask why, and whether the cause is transferable.
3. **Business-model questions**, one or two per run, rotating: gifting and
   registries; subscriptions on consumables; private label (Kuling, Stoy)
   share and margin; second-hand / resale; seasonal timing; B2B (nurseries);
   Lekmer vs. Babyshop overlap and cannibalisation.

Everything that looks promising but cannot be tested with today's data goes
under `needs_data` with the missing source named — that list drives which
data we add next.
