        How to read this
        Searchesview_search_results events: one per results page a visitor reaches by searching. Paging and filtering a result list are not counted again; a reload is.
        Search termWhat the visitor typed, lower-cased (the site may auto-correct it; the results shown are for the corrected query).
        Search rateSearches per 100 GA4 sessions in the same market and days. Searches cover every storefront, sessions the Babyshop one.
        Unique termsDistinct terms searched in the period.
        Brand searchThe term contains, as whole words, a brand with product views on the site in the last 120 days.
        Rising / newRising: at least 10 searches and +50 % or more vs the previous period. New: not searched in the previous period. Shown only when the previous period lies entirely after tracking started.
        Other marketEvery GA4 country outside SE, NO, DK and FI (mostly unattributed traffic, GB, US).
        Data freshnessSnapshot written daily by the ecom-refresh job; periods end yesterday. GA4 revises the last ~3 days.
        Not coveredAutocomplete clicks that go straight to a product page, searches that leave before results load, visitors who block GA4. Search → purchase is not linked in this data.
