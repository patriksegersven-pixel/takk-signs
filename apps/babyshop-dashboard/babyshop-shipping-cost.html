What outbound freight actually costs per order, market, delivery method and order — estimated per order from the delivery choice in Norce and the basket's weight and volume, priced with each carrier's own rates and invoice lines, and calibrated every week against the carriers' invoices in Business Central. Outbound freight only; return freight and customs are not included. Pick & pack and packaging have their own section, kept apart from freight.

      Per market

      How it is calculated
      Carrier and method
      From the delivery mode the customer chose at checkout (Norce, via Ingrid). Orders without one (before mid-June 2026) get the market's usual method; a Swedish item of 20 kg or more goes Home Delivery.
      Basket weight and size
      Gross weight and package dimensions per item from Norce, times quantity. DHL and Bring bill on volume when the parcel is light but bulky, so size matters as much as weight.
      Price per order
      Bring, Posti and Airmee: the amount on the carrier's invoice line for that order where it exists, otherwise the average actual cost for the market, method and weight band. DHL: what DHL billed per order for the same parcel volume (or weight), including split parcels and fuel. UPS: UPS's negotiated rate for the country, service and weight.
      Calibration
      Each week the estimate is scaled so each carrier's total matches its outbound freight booked in Business Central (account 4336) for the latest three fully invoiced months. Posti only counts the web-order share of its invoice; Airmee is not scaled (its fixed monthly amount is kept out of the per-order cost).
      Not included in freight
      Return freight (4338) and customs (4335). Pick & pack and packaging are shown in their own section and never added to the freight figures.
      Pick & pack
      Unique items in the order (distinct SKUs in Norce; several of the same item are charged once) × the PostNord TPL contract rate per unique item. Checked monthly against account 4497 (3PL outbound) on PostNord TPL's invoices; PostNord invoices once a month, for that month.
      Packaging
      Boxes and filler: account 4450 on PostNord TPL's invoices for the latest three invoiced months ÷ orders in those months, applied as a flat amount per order. Other suppliers' packaging costs are left out.
