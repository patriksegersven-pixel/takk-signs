How to read this

  • One list, no duplicates. Each opportunity is keyed by its mechanism and scope. When a later run finds it again, the row is updated instead of added.
  • GP2 / month is one driver (traffic, conversion, order value, repeat, margin, cost or recovered loss) applied to a baseline queried from the warehouse, as a low–base–high range. Nothing under kr/month at base case is listed.
  • Confidence reflects the kind of evidence: 80% when the effect was measured in our own data, 50% when inferred from a gap between our own markets or segments, 30% when the uplift is an outside benchmark.
  • Effort is person-weeks: S = 1, M = 3, L = 8, XL = 20. Score = GP2/month × confidence ÷ effort weeks.
  • Finland pilot shows what the base case is worth in Finland alone. Items marked Pilot are tested there for 4–6 weeks against SE/NO/DK before rollout; items marked Ship are no-regret fixes.
  • Rejected items stay rejected: a later run cannot bring one back.
